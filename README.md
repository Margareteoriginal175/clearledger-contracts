## 💡 What Is clearledger-contracts?

Think of clearledger-contracts as a digital paperwork system for invoices, built on top of Stellar, a network designed for fast, low-cost money movement. Instead of spreadsheets on four different computers, everything lives in one shared set of rules that every participant can see and trust.

The project is made up of four connected pieces:

- **Participant registry** – a clear list of everyone involved in the invoice process, such as the business sending the invoice, the customer paying it, and any funder stepping in to help with cash flow.
- **Invoice lifecycle** – a step-by-step record of each invoice, from the moment it is created to the moment it is settled, so nobody has to ask "where are we on this one?" again.
- **USDC liquidity pools** – USDC is a digital dollar. These pools let people who need money now connect with people who have money to lend, without a middleman taking a large cut.
- **Escrow coordination** – a neutral holding area for funds until agreed conditions are met, so both sides feel protected.

All of this runs on the Stellar test network, which means you can explore freely without risking real money.

## 🧾 What You Can Do With It

Here are the everyday things this software makes possible:
